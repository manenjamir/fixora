fixora App
fixora App is an on-demand, tech-enabled doorstep electronics and home appliance repair platform built specifically for underserved regional markets like Dimapur, Nagaland.

📌 Project Summary
fixora App eliminates the risk and hidden costs of traditional hardware maintenance by providing transparent, doorstep repair services. It replaces upfront diagnostic fees and extra transport charges with a risk-free check-up model, clear part-quality tiers, and real-time status tracking.

💡 Core Value Propositions
Free Doorstep Check-Up: Technicians inspect devices on-site at zero cost. If the customer declines the estimate, they owe ₹0.

Free Warehouse Logistics: Zero transport or delivery fees when a complex repair requires off-site warehouse service.

Transparent Repair Tiers: Customers select their preferred quality and budget level—A1 (OEM with 6-month warranty), A2 (First Copy with 3-month warranty), or A3 (Quick Fix with no warranty).

Repair Location Transparency: Diagnostic quotes explicitly declare upfront whether the job will be completed On-Site or transferred to the Warehouse before the user accepts.

Secure Handover: Job completions and warehouse deliveries require customer OTP verification to confirm satisfaction.

🛠️ Serviced Devices & Categories
Personal Electronics: Smartphones, laptops, and desktop PCs.

Home Appliances: Air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines, TVs, and fans.

Electrical Infrastructure: General electrical wiring and household electronic items.
